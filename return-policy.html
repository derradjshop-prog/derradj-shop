📋 Overview

At Derradj Shop, we are committed to delivering high-quality products to every customer across all 58 wilayas of Algeria. We take full responsibility when a product does not meet the expected standard due to a defect, damage during shipping, or an error on our part.

This page explains when returns and exchanges are accepted, the required time frame for submitting a request, what you need to provide, and how each case is handled. We aim to resolve every issue fairly, quickly, and without hassle.

✅ This policy applies to all orders placed through derradjshop.com and delivered within Algeria.

✅ When Returns Are Accepted

We accept return requests only in the following situations:

  • Defective product: The product has a manufacturing defect, a printing error, or a quality issue.
  • Damaged during delivery: The product arrived visibly damaged as a result of shipping.
  • Wrong product received: You received a different product from what you ordered.
  • Non-conforming product: The product does not match the description or specifications shown on the product page.

To be eligible, all of the following conditions must be met:

  1. You contacted us within 48 hours of receiving your order.
  2. The product is unused and in its original condition — original packaging, not damaged or altered by the customer.
  3. You provide clear photos or a short video clearly showing the problem (damage, defect, or wrong item).
💡 Tip: Photograph the package and product immediately upon receipt, especially if you notice any external damage. This helps us process your request faster.

🚫 When Returns Are Not Accepted

The following situations are not eligible for a return or exchange:

  • The customer changed their mind after receiving the product.
  • The product has been used, read, or is no longer in its original condition.
  • The product was damaged by the customer after delivery.
  • The return request was submitted after the 48-hour window from delivery.
  • The original packaging has been discarded, torn, or is missing.
⚠️ Please note: Returns are not accepted for personal preference or change of mind. We strongly encourage you to review all product details, descriptions, and photos carefully before placing your order. If you have any questions before ordering, contact us — we are happy to help you choose the right product.

📝 How to Submit a Return Request

If your order meets the conditions above, follow these steps to submit a return or exchange request:

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order — do not wait, as requests outside this window will not be accepted.
  2. Reach out via WhatsApp (fastest) or our Contact Page.
  3. Provide your order details: full name, phone number, product name, and order date.
  4. Attach clear photos or a video showing the issue (damage, defect, wrong item, etc.).
  5. Our team will review your request and respond with next steps within 24 hours.
💬 WhatsApp: +213 555 49 13 16 — the fastest way to reach our support team.

🔄 Exchange Policy

We accept product exchanges in the following cases:

  • The product you received is damaged or has a defect.
  • The product received is different from what you ordered.

If the error is on our side, we will arrange the exchange at no additional cost to you. A replacement will be dispatched once the original product is reviewed or returned, depending on the situation.

Incorrect delivery information:

If your order could not be delivered or was returned because of incorrect information provided at checkout (wrong address, wrong phone number, or the customer was unreachable), additional delivery fees may apply for re-shipping the order.

⚠️ Please double-check your name, phone number, and full address before confirming your order. Errors in delivery details are the customer's responsibility.

🛡️ Our Responsibility

Derradj Shop takes full responsibility in the following situations:

  • The product was damaged or defective before being dispatched.
  • An incorrect product was sent due to an error on our part.
  • The product does not match the description or specifications listed on the website.

In any of these cases, we will either replace the product free of charge or provide a full refund — whichever option you prefer.

❌ Products that were damaged by the customer after delivery, products that have been used or altered, and returns requested outside the 48-hour window are not covered by this policy and will not be accepted.

Need Help With Your Order?

Our team is ready to assist you. Reach out within 48 hours of receiving your order and we will resolve your issue as quickly as possible.
